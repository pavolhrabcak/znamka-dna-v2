Nyasaland, centred on Lake Nyasa (now Lake Malawi), became a British protectorate in 1891. David Livingstone's explorations in the region had drawn British attention, and the anti-slavery mission to the area preceded formal colonial rule.

The KGVI definitives of 1938, engraved and printed by Waterlow & Sons, depict the colonial badge and Lake Nyasa scenery. The territory's scenic stamps are well regarded by East African specialists.
