Bermuda, a group of small islands in the North Atlantic, became a British Crown Colony in 1684 and remains a British Overseas Territory. The islands were strategically important as a Royal Navy base and later as a transatlantic cable relay station.

Bermuda's stamps are highly regarded for their pictorial quality. The KGVI definitives of 1938, engraved and printed by De La Rue, feature striking views of Hamilton Harbour and the surrounding islands in two-colour recess printing.
