Singapore became a separate Crown Colony in 1946, having previously been part of the Straits Settlements. Founded as a British trading post by Stamford Raffles in 1819, it had grown into one of the most important commercial harbours in the world. The colony was occupied by Japan from 1942 to 1945, after which it was reconstituted separately from the rest of Malaya.

Singapore issued its own stamps from 1948. The QEII definitive series of 1955, printed by Bradbury, Wilkinson & Co., features views of the harbour and city. Singapore joined the Federation of Malaysia in 1963 but separated in 1965 to become an independent republic.
