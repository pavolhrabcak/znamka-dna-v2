British Somaliland on the Horn of Africa was a protectorate established in 1884 to protect British interests in the region, particularly the trade route to India via the Suez Canal. Berbera served as the main port and administrative centre.

The KGVI definitives of 1938, printed by De La Rue, feature the Berbera Blackhead sheep, oryx and local scenery — making them among the most distinctive of the East African colonial issues. The use of annas and rupees as currency (reflecting Indian influence) was unusual in British colonial philately.
