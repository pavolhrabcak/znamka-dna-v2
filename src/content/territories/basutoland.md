Basutoland, a landlocked territory entirely surrounded by South Africa, was placed under British protection in 1868 at the request of the Basotho chief Moshoeshoe. It remained a High Commission Territory outside the Union of South Africa until independence as Lesotho in 1966.

Stamps were not issued until 1933. The pictorial series depicts the distinctive conical Basotho hat, cattle and mountain scenery. The remote and land-locked nature of the territory makes its philatelic material less common than many other African colonies.
