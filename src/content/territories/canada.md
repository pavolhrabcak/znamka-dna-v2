Canada was a self-governing Dominion within the British Empire from 1867. The Statute of Westminster (1931) granted it full legislative independence, though it remained within the Commonwealth. Unlike a colony, Canada controlled its own postal administration and issued distinctive stamps reflecting its national identity.

The KGV era "Scroll" issue of 1928–1929 replaced the long-running "Admirals" definitive series. The 50c denomination, engraved by Waterlow & Sons, features the _Bluenose_ — a 258-ton Nova Scotian fishing and racing schooner built in 1921 that was undefeated in international racing for eighteen years. The Bluenose remains one of the most celebrated subjects in North American philately and appears today on the Canadian dime.
