St Kitts (St Christopher) was the first British colony in the Caribbean, settled in 1623. Together with the neighbouring island of Nevis, it formed one of Britain's oldest Caribbean possessions and was the starting point for British colonisation of the region.

The colony issued its own stamps from 1870. The pictorial definitives of the KGVI and QEII periods depict Brimstone Hill Fortress and local scenery, produced in recess by De La Rue and later Waterlow & Sons.
