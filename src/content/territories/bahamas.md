The Bahamas, an archipelago of some 700 islands in the western Atlantic, became a British Crown Colony in 1718 following the suppression of piracy. Nassau on New Providence island served as the colonial capital.

The colony's stamps are notable for their pictorial definitives depicting local industry, seascapes and the governor's residence. The KGVI series of 1938 was produced by De La Rue using recess printing on chalk-surfaced paper.
