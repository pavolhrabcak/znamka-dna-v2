Swaziland, a small landlocked kingdom in southern Africa, became a British protectorate in 1903. Like Basutoland and Bechuanaland, it was a High Commission Territory that maintained a separate identity from South Africa.

The 1938 KGVI definitives, printed by De La Rue, depict the Swazi shield and spears alongside the colonial badge. The QEII series from Waterlow & Sons continued the tradition of simple but elegantly engraved designs for this small territory.
