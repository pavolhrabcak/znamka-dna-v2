The Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland, also known as the Central African Federation, was formed in 1953 by joining Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia and Nyasaland. It was intended to create a strong multi-racial state in central Africa, but was dissolved in 1963 following nationalist opposition, particularly in Nyasaland.

The federation's stamps, produced by Waterlow & Sons, depict Victoria Falls and scenes from across the three territories. The short-lived federation lasted only a decade, and its stamps — particularly the 1959 pictorial definitives — are a compact collecting area.
