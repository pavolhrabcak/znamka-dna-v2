Sierra Leone became a British Crown Colony in 1808. Freetown was established as a settlement for freed slaves and became one of the most important centres of West African colonial administration and education.

The KGV pictorial definitives of 1932, engraved by Waterlow & Sons, depict rice harvesting and local economic scenes with exceptional recess engraving quality. The subsequent KGVI series from De La Rue maintained the high standard.
