The British Virgin Islands, comprising some 50 islands and cays in the northeastern Caribbean, have been a British territory since 1672. Road Town on Tortola is the capital. The islands have issued their own stamps since 1866.

The KGVI definitives of 1938 were produced by De La Rue using recess engraving and depict the local seal device. Later QEII issues from Waterlow & Sons continued the tradition of high-quality intaglio printing.
