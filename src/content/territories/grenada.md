Grenada, the southernmost of the Windward Islands, became a British colony following the Treaty of Paris in 1763. The island is known as the "Isle of Spice" for its production of nutmeg, mace and cloves, which feature in its colonial coat of arms.

The KGVI definitives of 1938, printed by De La Rue using recess engraving, depict scenes of Grand Anse Beach and the colonial badge. The designs capture the lush Caribbean scenery characteristic of Windward Islands stamp issues of the period.
