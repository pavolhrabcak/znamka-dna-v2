Dominica, the most mountainous island in the Eastern Caribbean, became a British colony in 1763 following the Treaty of Paris. The island's rugged interior, tropical forests and rivers are reflected in the scenic stamp designs issued throughout the colonial period.

The 1938 KGVI definitives, produced by De La Rue using recess engraving, are notable for their rich green colour schemes evoking the island's lush vegetation. The series depicts the Layou River, boiling lake and the botanical gardens at Roseau.
