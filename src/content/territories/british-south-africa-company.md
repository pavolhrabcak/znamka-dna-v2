The British South Africa Company, chartered by Queen Victoria in 1889, administered a vast territory in south-central Africa under Cecil Rhodes. The company issued its own stamps for Rhodesia (covering modern Zimbabwe and Zambia) from 1890 until it handed over administration in 1923–24.

BSAC stamps are notable for their elaborate engraved designs, particularly the Arms types and the Double Head issues under George V. These early recess-printed stamps, produced by Waterlow & Sons, Bradbury Wilkinson and De La Rue, are among the most elaborately designed of any British colonial series and are highly sought after by Rhodesia specialists.
