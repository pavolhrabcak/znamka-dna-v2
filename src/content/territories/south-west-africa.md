South West Africa (now Namibia) was a German colony until captured by South African forces in 1915. It was administered by South Africa under a League of Nations mandate from 1920.

Like South Africa itself, SWA stamps were issued in bilingual pairs with English and Afrikaans text. The pictorial definitives, particularly the 1931 series from Bradbury, Wilkinson, depict the dramatic landscapes of the territory including the Bogenfels sea arch, Windhoek and local wildlife.
