Bechuanaland Protectorate was established in 1885 to prevent German South West Africa and the Transvaal from linking up and blocking British expansion northward. Like Basutoland and Swaziland, it became a High Commission Territory rather than being incorporated into South Africa.

The colony's early stamps were overprints on Cape of Good Hope issues. Later pictorial series depicted local wildlife and Baobab trees. The KGVI definitives continued the tradition of simple portrait and badge designs produced by De La Rue.
