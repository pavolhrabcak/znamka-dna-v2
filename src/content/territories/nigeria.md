Nigeria was formed in 1914 by the amalgamation of the Northern and Southern Nigeria Protectorates under Sir Frederick Lugard. At independence in 1960, it was the most populous country in Africa. Lagos on the Gulf of Guinea served as the colonial capital.

The pictorial definitives of 1936–38, produced by De La Rue using recess engraving, depict scenes of Nigerian economic life: the River Niger, fishing villages, timber extraction and mining. These designs are celebrated for their documentary quality and fine engraving.
