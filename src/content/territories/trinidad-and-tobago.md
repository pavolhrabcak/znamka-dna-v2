Trinidad was captured from Spain in 1797 and Tobago was merged with it in 1889 to form the colony of Trinidad and Tobago. Port of Spain became the capital and a major commercial hub of the Caribbean.

The KGVI definitives of 1938, engraved and printed by De La Rue, depict oil derricks, First Boca (the northern strait) and the Imperial College of Tropical Agriculture. Oil wealth is reflected in the selection of motifs, marking Trinidad's transition from an agricultural to an industrial economy.
